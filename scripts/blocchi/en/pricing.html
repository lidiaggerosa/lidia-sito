Frequently asked questions

How much does artificial intelligence software for law firms cost?

It depends on the scope of use and the provider. Lidia starts at €125 a month, and Lidia Professional includes every feature, every area of law and access to official legislative and case-law sources. Many tools in the sector instead sell by practice area, keep the most useful features for higher tiers, or require a separate legal database subscription: these are the three things to check when comparing.

Are there modules to buy for each practice area?

No. Lidia Professional covers every area of law: there are no practice-area packages to add.

Do I have to pay to try Lidia?

No. The trial lasts seven days, is free and runs on the firm's real documents, not on a pre-packaged demo. The quote comes after the trial.

How does a legal database differ from legal AI in terms of cost?

They are two different costs because they do two different things: a database gives access to sources; legal AI works on the sources and on the firm's own documents, covering research, case-file analysis and drafting. With Lidia, access to official sources is included in the subscription: it is not an added cost. The comparison should be made on the work they take off your hands, not on the subscription fee.
