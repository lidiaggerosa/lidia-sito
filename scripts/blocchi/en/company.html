Who's behind Lidia

Lidia S.r.l. builds legal intelligence that works with you: it researches, analyses, drafts and automates the most complex processes. There is one product, serving law firms, in-house legal departments and the insurance sector.

Designed by lawyers

The leadership team comes from leading Italian and international law firms, and the consequence is not a story: it is the way Lidia proposes to integrate AI into professional workflows.

Citing sources is something any tool does these days. That is not the point. Legal research is not the answer to a query: it is a structured analysis. It means defining the issue, distinguishing between lines of authority, weighing precedents and arriving at work that stands up in front of a colleague and in front of a judge. That is what Lidia delivers: structured output, not an answer, because the people who designed it have done that work.
