Why it matters

Real sources, not invented ones.

A general-purpose AI assistant can go wrong exactly where a lawyer cannot afford it: the sources.

The risk

On its own, the assistant can make things up.

It can cite laws and judgments that do not exist. In a pleading or an opinion, an invented reference is a mistake that falls on whoever signs it.

With Lidia

Every reference that comes from Lidia comes from a real source.

Legislation, case law and guidance come from the legal research Lidia carries out on official sources. They are always cited and verifiable: you open them and can consult them directly.

How to use it

In the assistant’s chat, just mention Lidia in your request. Lidia searches, works on your documents and hands the result back to the assistant with the references. The assistant carries on from there.

«Lidia, research the Court of Cassation’s case law on dismissal for just cause.»

A few examples

  • Lidia, what does the Civil Code say about limitation of liability in contracts?
  • Lidia, in the Supplier X matter, which termination clauses are in the contracts?
  • Lidia, start the analysis of this judgment.
  • Lidia, run a red flag report on the contract.

Your data

How data is protected →

  • You sign in with your Lidia account. The assistant only sees what you see in Lidia.
  • Documents stay in Lidia. The assistant only receives what the request needs.
  • You can disconnect Lidia at any time.
  • Lidia’s safeguards stay the same. AWS, data in the EU, ISO/IEC 27001, CSA STAR Level 1.
  • The conversation goes through the assistant you choose. Under the terms of your Claude or ChatGPT plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is an MCP server?

MCP is an open standard that connects an AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT, to other tools. The Lidia MCP server is the link between your assistant and Lidia.

Why use Lidia inside my AI assistant?

Because a general-purpose AI assistant has no access to official-source databases and no legal context consistent with the work legal professionals do. With Lidia it works on real legislation, case law, guidance and matters, always citing its sources.

How do I call Lidia?

Just mention it in your request, for example: «Lidia, research the Court of Cassation’s case law on dismissal for just cause».

Which Lidia features can I use?

All of them: legislation and case-law research, questions on documents, workflows, matters and archive.

Does what I do in the assistant stay in Lidia?

Yes. The matters, documents and notes you save from the assistant are there in Lidia.

Which AI assistants does it work with?

Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP-compatible AI assistants.

Does Lidia stop the assistant from inventing sources?

Laws and judgments that come from Lidia come from its legal research on official sources, and they are always cited: you can open and check each one.

Do I have to stop using Lidia on the platform?

No. Lidia is still available on the platform and in Word.
