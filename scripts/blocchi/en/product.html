Frequently asked questions

What is artificial intelligence software for law firms?

It is a system that applies artificial intelligence to the core activities of a firm: research on official legislative and case-law sources, analysis of case files and contracts, assisted drafting. It differs from a general-purpose assistant because it works on the firm's own documents, cites the sources it uses and makes every step verifiable. Lidia is AI software for law firms, with data kept in the European Union.

Which legislative sources does Lidia use?

Italian and EU primary legislation, and secondary regulation from sector authorities: Bank of Italy, IVASS, CONSOB. Official sources are included in the subscription, with no separate database subscriptions.

Which case-law sources does Lidia use?

The Italian Supreme Court of Cassation (Joint Divisions; Civil, Criminal, Labour and Tax Divisions); the Constitutional Court, the Court of Auditors, the Court of Justice of the EU and the European Court of Human Rights; the Council of State, the Regional Administrative Courts (TAR) and first- and second-instance tax courts; a selection of judgments from Courts of Appeal and ordinary Courts.

What is GraphRAG and why does it matter in legal research?

GraphRAG is an information retrieval technique that, besides finding documents similar to the question, reconstructs the relationships between the entities involved: a provision, the judgments applying it, the related precedents. In law this matters because the useful answer is rarely an isolated document: it is the chain that links them. Lidia uses GraphRAG for research on official sources.

Does Lidia work inside Microsoft Word?

Yes. Documents are drafted and reviewed in Word through an add-in: Lidia's analysis and drafts arrive in the file the firm is already working on, with no exports or copy-pasting.
