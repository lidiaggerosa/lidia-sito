Every feature of Lidia, AI software for law firms and in-house legal teams, is built around the professional's work, from the simplest task to the most complex process. Control over answers and outputs stays with you, at every step.

## More features


### Web research

Industry analysis, in-depth research, online sources: Lidia's agents search the web without leaving the platform and bring the results into the matter's context.

Precise questions across the firm's entire archive. The answer shows the documents it comes from.
