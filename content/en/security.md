# Where your clients' documents end up

It is the first question a partner asks, and the right one. A case file is not just a file: it is material covered by professional secrecy, and whoever handles it is accountable. This page answers point by point, with no referral to a privacy notice to read later.


## In the European Union, and it stays there

**AWS infrastructure in the European Union**. Documents are encrypted **at rest and in transit** and never leave the European Union.

It is the minimum requirement for an Italian firm to entrust material covered by professional secrecy to an AI system: the data stays where European law protects it.


## Model providers see nothing

**No firm data** is transmitted to or retained by language model providers, and none is used to train their models.

That is the difference between using a general-purpose assistant and using a system built for legal work: with the first, your case file leaves your perimeter; with the second, it does not.


## What they certify, one line each


### ISO/IEC 27001

The information security management system is certified by a third party, not self-declared.


### CSA STAR Lvl. 1

Cloud infrastructure security is documented to the Cloud Security Alliance standard.


### ISO 9001

Business processes are certified for quality and traceability.


### GDPR

Processing compliant with Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
